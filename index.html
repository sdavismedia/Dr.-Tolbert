What This Is

More Than an Interview—A Platform for Visibility, Credibility, and Protection

The Legacy & Law Interview Series with Dr. Willie Tolbert is a curated spotlight for women entrepreneurs who are serious about their next level.

This interview experience is designed to:

  • Highlight your story and the impact of your business
  • Showcase the milestone moments that prove your growth
  • Create visibility around the work you do and the people you serve
  • Open a powerful conversation around legal protection, brand security, and building with wisdom

Every featured guest will have the opportunity to share her business journey, her mission, and why this stage of growth requires more than passion—it requires protection.

Who This Is For

This Opportunity Is for Serious Entrepreneurs

To ensure this platform remains high-quality and aligned with business owners who are truly building, applicants should meet the following criteria:

  • You have an active, registered business
  • Your business has been operating for at least 1 year
  • You have an active website or professional social media landing page
  • You are currently serving clients, selling products, or growing your business intentionally
  • You are ready to share both your progress and your vision publicly
This is not for hobbyists. This is for women building businesses with purpose, momentum, and a vision for legacy.
